# Demo: Liwe

**About 60 seconds.** `npm run dev` → http://localhost:3000

The app is the Liwe wizard (sage green, beige). Landing, then `/pilot`.

1. **`/`** — headline, three figures (17.9% vs 31.3%, X-ray yes CT no, ≤ 10 days). Click **Design a 12-month pilot**.
2. **County** — Kainuu is selected. Continue.
3. **Direct CT** — Yes.
4. **Who** — Satakunta-style.
5. **Scans** — pick a number that covers the stated need (not +0).
6. **GP wait** — keep today’s wait.
7. **Result** — protocol: comparator, slots/week, ≤ 10 days, stop rule, Guldbrandt line. **Print / PDF** if you want a page.

**Fail beat:** go back to Who, choose Broader, then Scans **+0**. The brief should say the route is over the share limit (or that patients queue). Do not use Helsinki just because it is large.

Do not say lives saved, stage shift, or that the 14 and 55 day figures are national. They are Satakunta 2021–22, one study (Z13). The 14-day GP appointment figure is Z12, a news summary.
