# Idea — Liwe

**Problem:** Finland has the lowest Nordic 5-year cancer survival for key poor-prognosis cancers (esp. lung). County maps look “equal,” but patients lose time at the **primary-care imaging gate**, and language/education gaps drive unequal early detection.

**Insight:** Equality between wellbeing services counties ≠ equal chance for every person. The fix is a **shared pathway intervention**, not another regional league table.

**Solution:** A digital twin of the detection pathway that lets leaders simulate scaling Satakunta’s primary-care direct chest CT model under CT/staff constraints, then export a sourced action brief toward National Cancer Strategy Goal 3.

**Edge for Junction:** Sharp problem → located bottleneck → implementable recommendation → legal-safe (no patient AI) → recordable 90s demo.
